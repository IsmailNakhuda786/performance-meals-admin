PERFORMANCE MEALS ADMIN PLATFORM

Design a complete enterprise SaaS Admin Portal for Performance Meals, a meal subscription and ready-meal delivery company.

CRITICAL REQUIREMENT:

Ready Series and Meal Plans are two separate business units.

They must NEVER be merged operationally.

Separate:
- Orders
- Production Queues
- Dispatch Queues
- Revenue Reporting
- Subscriber Management

Shared departments may view both businesses but must always see them separated visually and operationally.
